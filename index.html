DESKTOP CONTROLS

A little help.

Open the How to Play slideshow for a short walkthrough of casting, reeling, fish tugs, boating and island progression. Replay it here any time.

Boating: tap Board boat / Return to boat, or press R, from anywhere when not fishing. You return to your own parked boat; it never travels to a selected island for you. W/S accelerates/reverses; A/D steers (or use the joystick). Q stops to fish; when stopped, move around the deck with WASD / joystick, and Q returns to the helm. E docks when nearby. Any boat can enter any level-unlocked territory; locked territories have invisible circular barriers. Click the minimap or M for the full chart and a waypoint.

Shore luck: −95% within 6m of shore, gradually recovering to full luck at 48m. Luck is measured at your position when you release the cast, not the bobber—take the boat offshore. Between territories, cruise onward to find fishing waters.

Hold fish from Bag → Fish; they stay there until sold at a market. Equip owned tackle anywhere. New gear is sold at local stalls.

For a clean screenshot, frame your shot, then choose Hide HUD. Tap or click anywhere to return—your first tap only restores the interface.

Use Tab to navigate panels, and Left / Right or Home / End to switch Bag tabs.
